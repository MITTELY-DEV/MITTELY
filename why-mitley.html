Our promise

Why MITTELY

Built for creators who care about quality, speed, and fair pay.

Curated quality

Every product is reviewed by our team before it reaches the store. No filler, no broken files, no low-effort templates. Only resources we'd use ourselves.

Instant delivery

Pay once, download immediately. Signed URLs keep your files secure, and you get lifetime access to every update the designer ships.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you buy or sell.

You get the full source files listed on the product page — typically Figma files, HTML/CSS/JS templates, or both. Every purchase includes a license for the scope you selected and lifetime access to updates.

The Standard license covers use in one end product, personal or commercial, and cannot be resold. The Extended license covers one end product sold to one client, or unlimited personal projects, and still cannot be resold as-is. Full terms are on the license page.

Right after payment you're redirected to a success page with download buttons. You can also access every purchase from your dashboard at any time. Downloads use signed URLs that expire after 60 seconds for security.

Prices are stored in USD and can be displayed in USD ($) or Ghanaian Cedi (₵ GHS). The final charge is made in whichever currency you select at checkout, using a live exchange rate.

Because our products are digital downloads delivered instantly, we don't offer refunds after download. If a file is broken or not as described, contact us and we'll make it right.

Fill out the form on the Sell with us page. We review every submission and get back to you within a few days. If accepted, we'll help you publish your first product.
